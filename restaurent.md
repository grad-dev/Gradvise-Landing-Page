> **Purpose:** This document gives an AI agent full context about the product, its revenue philosophy, target customer, and feature set — to assist in building, writing, or designing the landing page.

## 🎯 What Is This Product?

A **restaurant operating system** — not just a menu tool or a QR code generator.

It's an all-in-one platform that helps restaurant owners **make more money from every customer they already have**, while cutting the operational chaos that leaks revenue every day.

The core promise: **Turn your restaurant into a revenue machine — not just a place that takes orders.**

---

## 💰 Revenue Philosophy (The North Star)

Everything in this product is designed around one question:

> *"How does this feature help the restaurant owner earn more?"*

Revenue grows in exactly **4 ways** for any restaurant. This platform attacks all four:

| Revenue Lever | How the Platform Helps |
|---|---|
| **1. More customers walk in** | Marketing campaigns, Google review nudges, online presence, referral programs |
| **2. Each customer spends more** | Upsell engine, combo builder, modifier suggestions, loyalty tiers, tip prompts |
| **3. Customers come back more often** | CRM segmentation, automated re-engagement campaigns, loyalty stamps/points |
| **4. Less revenue leaks out** | Inventory cost tracking, staff performance, fewer missed orders, no-show prevention |

---

## 👤 Target Customer

**Primary:** Independent restaurant owner (1–3 locations) who is currently using a mix of:
- A basic QR menu tool (like ChoiceQR or similar)
- WhatsApp to handle orders or complaints
- A spreadsheet for customer data (or nothing at all)
- Zero marketing automation

**Pain Points They Feel Every Day:**
- "I don't know who my regular customers are"
- "I run discounts but can't tell if they worked"
- "My best customers disappear and I have no way to reach them"
- "I'm losing money on slow Tuesday afternoons and don't know what to do"
- "My staff forgets orders, and I have no visibility"

**What They Want:**
- More tables filled, more often
- Customers who come back without being chased
- Visibility into what's actually working
- Something simple enough for a busy owner to use

## 🏗️ Product Architecture (5 Phases)

### 🔴 Phase 1 — Core Operations (The Foundation)
> **Goal:** Replace basic tools. Make daily operations smooth.

- **Menu Management** — Visual menu builder with photos, videos, variants, modifiers, allergen tags, availability toggles, QR code generation
- **Customer Ordering** — Mobile-first ordering UI, cart, live order status, table auto-detection via QR
- **Table Management** — Visual floor plan, table status tracking, QR per table
- **Payments** — Stripe, Apple Pay/Google Pay, split bill, tip suggestions, GST invoicing
- **Basic Analytics** — Revenue dashboard, top items, peak hour heatmap, live order feed
- **Staff Order Screen** — KDS view, order accept/reject, waiter request handling

### 🟠 Phase 2 — Retention & Revenue Engine
> **Goal:** Turn one-time visitors into regulars. Launch campaigns that actually drive revenue.

- **Customer Intelligence (CRM)** — Auto-profiles built from order history. Segments: Premium, Regular, New, Lapsed, At-Risk
- **Campaign Engine** — WhatsApp/SMS/Email campaigns. Triggered by: lapsed visit, birthday, slow hours, post-meal, abandoned order
- **Feedback & Reputation** — Post-meal emoji rating, negative feedback → manager alert, positive → Google Review nudge
- **Loyalty & Rewards** — Stamp cards, points system, tiered loyalty, promo codes, birthday rewards, referral program

### 🟡 Phase 3 — Intelligence & Stickiness
> **Goal:** Give owners data they can act on. Reduce cost leakage.

- **Inventory Management** — Recipe costing, auto-deduction on orders, low stock alerts, margin % per dish
- **Staff Management** — Role-based access, shift scheduling, performance metrics, payroll export
- **Advanced Analytics** — Menu gap analysis, churn prediction, campaign ROI, revenue forecasting

### 🟢 Phase 4 — Scale & Expansion
> **Goal:** Grow to multiple locations. Capture online revenue.

- **Multi-Location Management** — Centralized dashboard, branch-wise menus/pricing, consolidated reporting
- **Online Presence & Delivery** — Auto-generated restaurant website, branded ordering page, delivery zone setup, marketplace integrations (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo)
- **Reservations** — Booking widget, automated reminders, waitlist, deposit collection, no-show tracking

### 🔵 Phase 5 — AI & Moat Features
> **Goal:** Personalization and intelligence that competitors can't copy quickly.

- **AI Features** — Personalized menus per returning customer, smart upsell engine, combo auto-generator, demand forecasting, sentiment analysis, smart campaign timing
- **Integrations** — Toast, Square, QuickBooks, Xero, Razorpay, WhatsApp Business API, Zapier, public REST API

## 🔑 Key Differentiators (Landing Page Hooks)

These are the angles that make the product stand out vs basic QR menu tools:

### 1. "Your menu is not just a menu — it's a sales engine"
Upsell suggestions, combo builder, modifier prompts, and AI-driven item sorting all push average order value up automatically.

### 2. "Know every customer by name (even if they never told you)"
The CRM auto-builds customer profiles from QR scan + order data. No signup friction. Owners wake up to a database of their regulars with visit history, spend, and risk signals.

### 3. "Send the right message at exactly the right moment"
Campaigns triggered by real behaviour — not just a bulk SMS blast. "Riya hasn't visited in 18 days → send her a personalised offer." Zero manual work.

### 4. "Turn a bad review into a private conversation. Turn a good one into a Google review."
Negative feedback is intercepted before it goes public. Positive customers are nudged to leave a Google review. Reputation managed on autopilot.

### 5. "Know your real margin — not just your revenue"
Recipe costing + inventory auto-deduction tells owners exactly which dishes make money and which ones quietly drain the kitchen budget.

### 6. "Fill your slow Tuesday afternoons automatically"
Slow hours detection triggers flash deal campaigns to nearby/lapsed customers. AI picks the best send time per customer.

## ✍️ Tone & Messaging Guide

**Voice:** Confident, practical, owner-first. Not tech-heavy. Not startup-jargon.

**Avoid:** "Leverage synergies", "cutting-edge AI", "revolutionary platform"

**Use instead:** "Fill more tables", "Bring back lapsed customers automatically", "Know your real margin", "Works while you sleep"

**The owner's emotional state we're speaking to:**
- Slightly overwhelmed
- Wants control and clarity
- Doesn't trust software that "does too much"
- Responds to specific, believable outcomes — not vague promises

**Headline formula that works:**
> [Specific outcome] + [how / without friction] + [for restaurant owners]

Examples:
- "Turn first-time visitors into regulars — automatically"
- "Fill slow afternoons with a single campaign"
- "Know which dishes make money. Cut the ones that don't."
